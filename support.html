Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → select Nook → Cancel Subscription. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account at any time from inside Nook. Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your journal entries and account information from our servers. The action cannot be undone.

How does Nook work?

Each day, Nook invites you to record a short voice check-in — just speak naturally about how you're feeling. Your recording is transcribed and turned into a personal journal entry, giving you a quiet place to reflect on your day over time.

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

Your journal entries are stored securely and are never shared with third parties or used to train any AI models. Voice recordings are transcribed and then discarded — only the text of your entry is kept. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
